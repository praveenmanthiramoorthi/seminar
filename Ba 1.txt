EXPERIMENT 7 – BIVARIATE AND MULTIVARIATE STATISTICAL ANALYSIS

AIM

To analyze the given dataset using Bivariate and Multivariate Statistical Analysis.

ALGORITHM

Bivariate Analysis – Scatter Plot

1. Open the dataset in Excel.


2. Go to Data → Data Analysis.


3. Select Bivariate Analysis.


4. Select two variables, Study Hours and Marks.


5. Select Scatter Plot.


6. Select the input range.


7. Choose the output range or new worksheet.


8. Click OK.


9. Observe the scatter plot and identify the relationship between the variables.



Multivariate Analysis – PCA 10. Select three variables: Study Hours, Attendance and Marks. 11. Go to Data → Data Analysis. 12. Select Multivariate Analysis / PCA. 13. Select the input range. 14. Choose the output range or new worksheet. 15. Click OK. 16. Observe the PCA output and identify the major components.

SAMPLE DATASET

Study Hours	Attendance	Marks

2	65	45
3	70	52
4	75	60
5	80	68
6	85	75
7	90	82
8	92	88
9	95	93


OUTPUT

Bivariate Analysis:
Scatter plot of Study Hours vs Marks showing a positive relationship.

Multivariate Analysis:
PCA output for Study Hours, Attendance and Marks, showing the principal components and the variation explained by them.

RESULT

Thus, Bivariate and Multivariate Statistical Analysis was successfully performed on the given dataset using Scatter Plot and PCA, and the relationships and patterns among the variables were identified.
